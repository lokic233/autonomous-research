**NEW THESIS 3:**

**One-sentence thesis:** A KV cache can deliver prefix *sharing without paged-attention indirection* — branches share physical pages over a single contiguous virtual address (0% attention overhead) and a divergent write copies exactly one page regardless of branch count or context length — a (share-without-paging) + (O(1-page) divergence) pair that neither paged nor contiguous prior systems achieve together.

**Built on which surviving asset (cite number):** Asset 1 (Metric 5c: exactly 1 page copied on shared-page overwrite, siblings bit-identical) + Asset 3 (Metric 3: ~0% attention overhead from contiguous-VA VMM pages), corroborated by Asset 5 (page swap changes attn output with pointer stable).

**Contribution type:** Primitive (a KV op: write-after-share at page granularity over contiguous VA).

**Closest prior work + surviving delta:** SGLang RadixAttention shares prefixes but *requires* a paged/block-table attention kernel (indexing indirection) and, on divergence, copies/recomputes at block granularity. vAttention has contiguous VA but no sharing/CoW at all. Surviving delta: ForkedKV is the only point that gets *both* — physical sharing across branches with a stable contiguous VA (so the attention kernel needs no block-table indirection, measured 5–22% kernel advantage vs FlashInfer after the honest Lab-3b retraction) AND in-place divergence at exactly 1 page. The claim is the *conjunction*, which is what's new.

**Highest-EV 30-day experiment:** Wire the write-after-share primitive into a real tree-shaped decode workload (beam search / self-consistency / tree-of-thought) on the existing N=4 multi-layer decode loop, and benchmark divergence cost and attention latency against (a) SGLang RadixAttention fork and (b) naive clone, across branch count and divergence depth. Prove the two-part claim: bytes-copied stays at 1 page per divergent write independent of N and context (already at N=84 elsewhere), AND end-to-end attention stays at the contiguous-VA latency (no paging indirection). **Why it is NOT one of the 6 dead theses:** Not "OS-style page-fault CoW" — divergence is *explicitly* triggered on write, no hardware GPU fault is claimed or needed. Not the "14x capacity win" — no capacity-vs-clone claim; the metric is bytes-per-divergent-write and attention indirection. Not "EDMM as a VMM mechanism" — the contribution is the share-without-paging *attention/memory pairing*, characterized against RadixAttention, not the cuMemMap call itself.

**Self-verdict:** YELLOW — the primitive is measured (1 page, bit-identical, 0% overhead), but the delta over RadixAttention is the conjunction + a modest 5–22% kernel edge; it needs a tree-decode workload where O(1-page) divergence visibly beats block-granular fork to clear the bar.
