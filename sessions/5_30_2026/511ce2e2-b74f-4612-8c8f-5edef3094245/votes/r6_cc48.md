### T-TAX FINAL

- **Vote: GREEN** (bounded — as a characterization/negative-result/decision-guidance contribution, not as a clean "pure-VMM mechanism" paper)

- **Is it MORE than A*+C* restated? — YES.** A* is the isolated *ceiling* (523,404 maps) and C* is the isolated *CoW slowdown* (1.06–2.20× vs SW, fork 240×) — both microbenchmarks of single mechanisms. E-T's new contribution is that the mechanisms **compound under realistic combined load on a real model**: fork+CoW+attention crashes at **~180–400 branches** vs the alias-only **523,404** ceiling — a **~1000× earlier** wall — because CoW remaps and the torch allocator share one finite per-context descriptor budget. And it converts "the mechanism is slow" into "the system loses" end-to-end: on Qwen2.5-7B layer-0, **SW wins at every B by 2.74×–10.62×, HW wins zero regimes** (≤136 vs 280→800 tok/s). A* and C* individually do not predict either the compounding or the empty e2e win-region; that is genuinely new, not a restatement.

- **Strongest remaining attack:** the throughput-collapse arm is **confounded by torch-allocator descriptor contention**, not proven to be a pure VMM property. If VMM CoW had a dedicated descriptor pool / VMM-aware allocator, the wall might move back from "a few hundred" toward 523K and throughput might recover — which would demote "*structurally* the wrong allocator" to "wrong allocator *as currently integrated with torch*." The word "structural" is only cleanly earned by the **ceiling** arm (vendor-specific: NVIDIA 523K, AMD no wall at 50M), not by the throughput arm. The 9–393 crash variance is also a confounded, not clean, measurement.

- **Venue: MLSys (best fit), ATC plausible.** A surprising, actionable, measured negative result ("don't use the HW feature everyone assumes is faster; SW dominates every regime") is exactly the MLSys/ATC characterization genre. **NOT OSDI/EuroSys as-is** — those reviewers will demand full-model (not single-layer), multi-GPU/multi-driver, and disentangling the torch confound before accepting "structural." Above workshop; the three-arm reproduction across A*/C*/E-T carries it past short-paper.

- **Honesty check:** GREEN on **measured** evidence, not hope — SW beats HW at every single measured B (2.74×–10.62×, zero HW wins), the 523,404 ceiling and 240× fork are independently reproduced GREEN, and the e2e collapse is on a real Qwen2.5-7B over 3 reps; the only thing I'm discounting is the *word "structural"* on the throughput arm, not the decision conclusion itself.
