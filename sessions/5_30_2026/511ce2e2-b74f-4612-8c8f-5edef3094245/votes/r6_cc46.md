### T-TAX FINAL

- **Vote: GREEN**

- **Is it MORE than A*+C* restated?** Yes. E-T's specific new contribution is the **compounding interaction** under realistic load: A* showed the ceiling exists at ~520K maps in isolation; C* showed VMM CoW is slower per-operation. E-T demonstrates that when CoW remaps and the torch allocator **share** the finite per-context descriptor budget, the usable wall drops from ~520K maps (alias-only) to crash-at-180–400-branches — a ~1000× reduction in effective headroom. The throughput data (SW 280→800 tok/s scaling vs HW capped ≤136 tok/s, 2.74×–10.62× ratio, 0/8 B-values won by HW) is a genuinely new end-to-end measurement that neither A* nor C* contained. A*+C* says "the parts are broken"; E-T says "the system is broken, and worse than the parts predict."

- **Strongest remaining attack:** The crash-branch variance (9–393) is the sharpest wedge. A reviewer can argue the 9-branch outlier reflects torch-allocator non-determinism, not a clean VMM property, and that the "structurally wrong" claim is partially confounded by the runtime sharing the descriptor namespace. Counter: the thesis explicitly scopes to "realistic fork+CoW+attention load" — torch *is* the realistic runtime, and unpredictable crashes between 9 and 393 branches are arguably *more* damning for production adoption than a clean deterministic wall. But a hostile R2 will call it "system integration noise, not architectural characterization." The honest rebuttal is strong (single-layer is VMM's *best* case; more layers consume more descriptors, moving the wall closer), but the paper must preempt this explicitly.

- **Does the negative result clear a real venue?** **MLSys or ATC** — yes. The three-arm evidence structure (latency + capacity + throughput), clean decision conclusion, and direct relevance to the agentic-serving design space fit MLSys's characterization track well. ATC accepts systems measurement papers with actionable guidance. OSDI/EuroSys would want multi-GPU / full-model / proposed-alternative-design — this is a characterization paper, not a new system, so those are a reach unless bundled with a design contribution. Not a workshop-tier result: the E-T compounding interaction and the "zero win-regime" sweep are substantive enough for a full paper.

- **Honesty check:** GREEN on measured evidence. The 0/8 win-regime sweep, 2.74×–10.62× throughput ratios, and crash-before-alias-ceiling compounding are all cited numbers from controlled experiments with 3-rep medians. The crash variance is messy but honestly bounded and, if anything, strengthens the practical indictment. I am not voting GREEN on hope — I am voting GREEN because E-T closes the gap between "VMM has bad microbenchmark properties" (A*+C*) and "VMM is unusable for the target workload class" (T-TAX), and that gap is the thesis's actual contribution.
